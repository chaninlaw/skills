# Clause checklist (small software vendor)

Defaults for a small vendor selling custom development plus maintenance. Each is a starting position; the agreed deal overrides it.

| Clause | Default |
|---|---|
| Parties and recitals | Legal names and IDs from `seller.md` and the client profile; the proposal and quotation version attached as the SOW |
| Services and deliverables | Each deliverable with its phase and target date; SOW attached and controlling for scope |
| Client responsibilities | Decision maker, data, access, content, UAT testers with dates; client delays move the timeline |
| Payment | Deposit on signing, then per accepted phase (e.g. 40/40/20); tied to deliverables, not dates; late-payment interest per local law; work may pause after a stated overdue period |
| Acceptance | Client accepts or rejects in writing within 5-10 business days with reasons tied to the acceptance criteria; silence means accepted; bugs found later go to warranty |
| Change requests | Written request; impact on scope, time and cost within a few business days; approved in writing before work; hourly rate stated |
| Revisions | Included UI rounds per `seller.md` (e.g. 2), then hourly |
| IP | Custom code assigned to the client on full payment; the vendor keeps pre-existing and reusable components and grants a perpetual, royalty-free licence to them; open-source keeps its own licences; portfolio mention unless the client objects |
| Confidentiality | Mutual, 2-5 years after the end; trade secrets for as long as they stay secret |
| Warranty | Fix material defects against the acceptance criteria for 30-90 days after acceptance; otherwise as-is; no warranty for changes made by others |
| Maintenance (MA) | Separate schedule: term (1 / 2 / 3 years), fee, included hours or tickets, response times by severity, what is excluded (new features, third-party outages), rollover yes / no, rate beyond, renewal and notice (30 days) |
| Hosting and third parties | Who pays and owns cloud accounts, domains, licences; vendor is not liable for third-party outages |
| Data protection | Processing terms whenever personal data is handled (local data-protection law) |
| Liability | Capped at fees paid under the contract (higher only for high-risk work); no indirect or consequential loss |
| Termination | For cause with a cure period (e.g. 14 days); for convenience with notice (30 days), paying for work done; handover of work in progress after payment |
| Non-solicitation | Optional, short (e.g. 12 months), both ways |
| General | Entire agreement; changes in writing only; independent contractor; force majeure; notices; governing law and disputes per jurisdiction |

## Tone

Plain words, obligations running both ways, no all-caps blocks. A contract the client can read is signed faster.
