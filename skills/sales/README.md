# Sales

Skills for selling custom software and maintenance as a freelancer or small software house: lead to signed contract, with every stage advanced by evidence in a per-project `docs/sales/` workspace. Not promoted yet: not shipped in the Claude Code plugin and not listed in the top-level `README.md` until tested on real deals.

Start with `/setup-sales-skills`, then `/ask-sales` whenever you are unsure what comes next.

## User-invoked

Reachable only when you type them (Claude Code: `disable-model-invocation: true`).

- **[setup-sales-skills](./setup-sales-skills/SKILL.md)**: Scaffold the `docs/sales/` workspace: conventions, seller profile, brand (CI) and branded document template, document storage target, then open the first client.
- **[ask-sales](./ask-sales/SKILL.md)**: Router over the sales skills: which one fits a situation, or where a deal stands, what is missing, and the next action.

## Model-invoked

- **[sales-research](./sales-research/SKILL.md)**: Open a client and deal: public company research with every fact sourced, and a folder for the client's documents.
- **[sales-discovery](./sales-discovery/SKILL.md)**: Prepare a discovery call, then turn the notes into a quoted discovery record and a recap the client confirms.
- **[sales-qualify](./sales-qualify/SKILL.md)**: Bid / no-bid, TOR requirement fit, and red flags graded by evidence.
- **[sales-solution](./sales-solution/SKILL.md)**: Scope, phases, estimate ranges, assumptions, and an optional paid discovery or POC.
- **[sales-pricing](./sales-pricing/SKILL.md)**: Cost floor, value ceiling, phase and MA-term options, and a conservative business case.
- **[sales-proposal](./sales-proposal/SKILL.md)**: Client-facing proposal, quotation and SOW, plus the follow-up plan.
- **[sales-document](./sales-document/SKILL.md)**: Render a sales document on-brand to versioned HTML and A4 PDF and deliver it to the storage target.
- **[sales-negotiate](./sales-negotiate/SKILL.md)**: Objection diagnosis, give/get plan, packages and walk-away.
- **[sales-contract](./sales-contract/SKILL.md)**: Contract draft or review against a clause checklist, then the delivery handoff.
