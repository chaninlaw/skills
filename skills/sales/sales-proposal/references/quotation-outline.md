# Quotation outline

A quotation (ใบเสนอราคา) is the priced, signable summary of the proposal. Values come from `seller.md` and `04-pricing.md`; the proposal and quotation must agree to the unit.

## Header

- Seller: legal name, address, tax ID (or personal ID rules for a freelancer, per `seller.md`), contact.
- Client: legal name, address, tax ID if known, contact person.
- Document number (numbering pattern from `seller.md`), issue date, valid until (validity from `seller.md`), reference to the proposal version.

## Lines

| # | Description | Qty | Unit | Unit price | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- Group by phase; MA on its own lines with its term and monthly or yearly amount.
- Optional items listed after the subtotal, clearly marked as not included in the total.

## Totals

- Subtotal
- Discount (only if traded for something; name what)
- Amount before VAT
- VAT at the rate in `seller.md`, or a line stating the seller is not VAT-registered
- Grand total, in figures and, where customary in the document language, in words (e.g. Thai baht text)
- Withholding tax the client deducts and the net payable, when `seller.md` says it applies

## Terms

- Payment schedule tied to deliverables (e.g. deposit on signing, then per accepted phase).
- Validity, delivery estimate, what the price assumes (link to the proposal's assumptions).
- Bank details for transfer.

## Acceptance

Two signature blocks (client approves / seller), with name, title, date. A signed quotation returned by the client is evidence for the negotiate stage.
