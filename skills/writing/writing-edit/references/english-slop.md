# English slop patterns

For English posts and the English passages in Thai ones. Fix the sentence, do not just delete the phrase.

- **Announcing**: "In this post, we'll explore...", "Let's dive in." Start with the first real sentence.
- **Era framing**: "In today's fast-paced world..." Delete.
- **Inflation**: delve, leverage, robust, seamless, game-changer, crucial, pivotal, unlock, elevate, landscape, realm, tapestry. Use the plain word or the number.
- **Manufactured contrast**: "It's not just X, it's Y." Say Y.
- **Triplets for rhythm**: "fast, simple, and secure" when one is backed. Keep the backed one.
- **Telling the reader how to feel**: "Interestingly,", "Notably,", "It's worth noting that". State the thing.
- **Hedging what the author knows**: "may potentially", "in some cases", "it depends". Commit, or say what it depends on.
- **Colon-headline bullets in prose**: "**Performance:** ..." Write the paragraph.
- **Em dashes everywhere**: use a comma, a period or parentheses.
- **Summary that repeats the post**: "In conclusion, we've seen..." End on the last real point.
- **Sign-offs**: "Happy coding!", "I hope this helps." Delete.
